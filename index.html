Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Welcome to the Cliff View Golf app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide to create an account, complete a booking, or purchase a membership:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional, if you provide one)
  • Account credentials (email and password) used to sign in
  • Booking and membership details, including date, time, number of golfers, membership tier, and amount paid
  • Purchase records, including a payment confirmation ID from our payment processor

We do not collect your full payment card number, CVV, or bank account details. Card and Apple Pay information is entered in Square’s secure payment sheet and is processed by Square. We only receive a one-time payment token and a payment confirmation ID.

We do not collect precise location, advertising identifiers, or analytics/tracking data for advertising.

How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process tee time bookings, tournament registrations, and membership purchases
  • Send booking and membership confirmation emails
  • Show your profile, bookings, and membership status in the app
  • Provide customer support and fix technical issues

We use this information for app functionality. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the app:

  • Supabase — account authentication and secure storage of profiles, bookings, and membership records
  • Square — payment processing
  • Resend — sending confirmation emails

These providers process data on our behalf to provide those services. Please review their privacy policies for more information.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures, including encrypted connections and restricted access to booking and account data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights (Colorado Privacy Act)

Colorado residents have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your personal data
  • Data portability
  • Opt-out of certain data processing

To exercise these rights, or to request that we delete your account and related booking information, contact us at info@visitcliffview.com.

Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following services:

  • Supabase (accounts and data storage)
  • Square (payments)
  • Resend (transactional email)

Children's Privacy

The Cliff View Golf app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Parents can contact us if they believe their child has submitted data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or the app, contact us at info@visitcliffview.com.
